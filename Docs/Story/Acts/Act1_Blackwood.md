# Act I — Blackwood

**Setting:** Blackwood, Wyoming · June 1862  
**Chapters:** 3 (MVP scope)  
**Central question:** Who killed Caleb Marsh, and why?

## Chapter summaries

### Chapter 1 — Arrival at Blackwood
Train car → Blackwood Station → Main Street (first block).  
Tutorial. Movement, interaction, dialogue introduced.  
Ends with: torn paper scrap matching the envelope. Name fragment: "...eb Marsh."  
Clues unlocked: `CalebMarshNameFragment`

### Chapter 2 — The Sawdust & Rye
Saloon and hotel.  
First moral choice (land dispute). June introduced. Dross's first appearance.  
Envelope opened — Pinkerton field cipher, partially decoded.  
Clues unlocked: `CalebMarshEnvelopeLetter`, `PinkertonCipher`, `JuneMarshdConnection`

### Chapter 3 — Dead Man's Errand
Outskirts → Harrow Mine (~1.5hr ride).  
Riding tutorial. Mine exploration. Boss: Two-Bit Terrence.  
Ledger recovered. June's quiet devastation at what it reveals.  
Act I ends: The Widow's letter arrives at the hotel.  
Clues unlocked: `HarrowMineLedger`, `ForgedLandDeeds`, `TheWidowLetter`
