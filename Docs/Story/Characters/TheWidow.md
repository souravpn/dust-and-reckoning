# "The Widow" — Character Brief

**Age:** Unknown  
**Role:** Act II antagonist (introduced Act I end)  
**Identity:** Unknown at Act I close

## What is known at Act I end

- She knows who Elias is
- She knows why he came to Blackwood
- She knew before he did
- She knows the same man who sent Elias killed Caleb Marsh

## Her letter (Act I closing beat)

> *"You found what Marsh died for. Now ask yourself who sent you to find it. The answer is the same man who killed him."*

## Design rule

Must feel dangerous before the player knows anything about her. Frightening because she is ahead of everyone.
