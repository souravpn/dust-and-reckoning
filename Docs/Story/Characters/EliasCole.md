## Backstory

Discharged from the Pinkertons involuntarily under circumstances not disclosed at game start. Travelling west on the Pacific Express carrying a sealed envelope addressed to a man in Blackwood whose name has been torn off.

## Arc

Arrived to disappear. Blackwood won't let him. By Act I's end he discovers he was placed in Blackwood deliberately — he is not a free agent in this story.
